Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Mindlayer does not collect, upload or sell any of your data.

Where your data lives

Your maps, attached files, settings and automatic backups are stored only in the app's own folder on your Mac. Mindlayer has no accounts and no servers.

Network

Mindlayer does not connect to the internet and contains no analytics, ads or crash reporting. It runs a local service that only your own Mac can reach (127.0.0.1:47321), so that AI tools you install yourself, such as Claude Code or Codex, can read and edit your maps. The service requires a token and cannot be reached from other devices.

AI tools

Mindlayer never sends your content to any AI service on its own. If you connect an AI tool, that tool can read the maps you let it access, and what it does with that content is governed by the tool's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Move a map to the Trash and empty it to delete that map. To remove everything, delete the app; its data folder is ~/Library/Containers/app.mindlayer.desktop.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email forwardthomasmiller@icloud.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

层思（Mindlayer）不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。

数据存在哪里

你的导图、挂的文件、设置和自动备份，都只保存在你自己 Mac 上 App 专属的文件夹里。层思没有账号系统，也没有服务器。

网络

层思不连接互联网，不含统计、广告或崩溃上报。它在本机开了一个只有这台 Mac 能访问的服务（127.0.0.1:47321），让你自己安装的 AI 工具（例如 Claude Code、Codex）读写你的导图。这个服务需要令牌才能访问，其他设备连不上。

AI 工具

层思不会主动把内容发给任何 AI 服务。你把 AI 工具连到层思后，它能读到你允许的导图，它怎么处理这些内容由它自己的隐私政策约束。

删除数据

把导图移到废纸篓再清空，就删掉了这张导图。想全部删除，删掉 App 即可；它的数据文件夹在 ~/Library/Containers/app.mindlayer.desktop。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，新版本会发布在这个页面，并更新生效日期。

联系我们

有隐私方面的问题，请发邮件到 forwardthomasmiller@icloud.com。
